Weekly Meal Prep System

Protein + veggies, remixed across rice, tortillas, and salad — without cooking every day.

The 3-Part System

  1. Two proteins, not one. Cook two different protein + veggie batches (e.g. lemon-herb chicken and ginger-soy tofu, or beef and chickpea curry) so you're never eating the same thing five days straight. Alternate them through the week.
  2. One big batch of rice. Cook fresh as needed in the rice cooker — no need to batch this ahead.
  3. One crunchy prep-ahead salad or slaw. Cabbage, carrots, and cucumber hold up far better over a week than lettuce. Keep the dressing in a separate small container and toss fresh each day so it doesn't wilt.

Keeping It From Feeling Repetitive

  • Keep 2-3 cheap flavor boosters on hand to add at serving time, not during cooking: lime, hot sauce, yogurt or sour cream, a drizzle of sesame oil, or fresh herbs. Same base, different meal.
  • Warm the tortilla in a dry pan for 30 seconds before filling — makes the wrap format feel distinct from "leftovers in bread."
  • Keep a small jar of quick-pickled onions or cucumbers (vinegar + salt + sugar, ready in an hour) for crunch and acidity across bowls, wraps, or the slaw.

Keeping It Healthy

  • Both proteins are lean-ish already; to cut fat further, swap to chicken breast or reduce oil by a tablespoon per batch — the sauce carries plenty of flavor.
  • The slaw/salad supplies a raw-veggie component daily that the cooked-veggie bowls alone don't — good for fiber and micronutrients you'd otherwise miss all week.
  • Swap rice half-and-half with cauliflower rice or a grain like farro for more fiber and fewer refined carbs, without changing anything else about the plan.

Sunday Prep Sequence

  1. Cook protein batch #1 (~25 min).
  2. Cook protein batch #2 while #1 cools (~25 min).
  3. Shred or slice slaw veggies and make the dressing (~15 min, can overlap with step 2).
  4. Portion proteins into containers; keep the slaw and dressing separate. Cook rice fresh each day in the rice cooker.
Total hands-on time: roughly 60-70 minutes, no rice batching needed since it's made fresh daily.
